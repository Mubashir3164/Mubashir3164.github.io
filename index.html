Research Highlights

bHLH Genes in Cotton

Genetic Diversity & Functional Significance

We performed a comprehensive genome-wide analysis of the Basic Helix-Loop-Helix (bHLH) transcription factor family in cotton. This study revealed key candidate genes involved in fiber development and elucidated their evolutionary divergence and functional roles.

Methods: Genome-wide Identification, Phylogenetic Analysis
